## 1. Mission Statement

> **Zawadi exists to ensure that no African student misses a life-changing scholarship opportunity because of information gaps, missed deadlines, or lack of application support.**

We believe talent is evenly distributed across Africa — but opportunity is not. A brilliant student in Nairobi, Lagos, Accra, or Addis Ababa should have the same shot at a fully-funded master's in London or a PhD in Toronto as a student who grew up knowing these opportunities exist. Zawadi closes that gap.

In Swahili, *zawadi* means "gift." We are the gift that stands between a capable African student and a missed opportunity that could have changed everything.

## 2. The Problem We Solve (Research-Backed)

### 2.1 Scale of the Problem

| Statistic | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| African students studying abroad | 400,000+ globally; 56,780 in US alone | UNESCO, IIE Open Doors 2024 |
| Nigerian students abroad (largest cohort) | ~85,000 (30% of African total) | WENR 2024 |
| Annual growth rate of African student mobility | 8-12% year-over-year | ICEF Monitor |
| Students who miss deadlines due to poor tracking | ~40% (self-reported) | Techsari survey (n=200) |
| Scholarship listings that are expired/misleading | ~35% | Internal audit of aggregator sites |

### 2.2 Six Core Problems

#### 2.2.1 Information Fragmentation
Scholarships for African students are scattered across 500+ university websites, government portals, foundation pages, and aggregator blogs. No single source reliably collects, verifies, and presents them in one place — filtered by eligibility. A student must check 15-20 websites weekly to stay current.

#### 2.2.2 Eligibility Blindness
Most global scholarship search engines don't filter by African nationality. Students waste hours reading listings they're not eligible for, while missing ones that specifically target them. Our research shows 70% of generic scholarship search results are irrelevant to any given African student.

#### 2.2.3 Deadline Chaos
Students track deadlines in spreadsheets, phone notes, or memory. Urgent deadlines are missed. Rolling deadlines are forgotten. The cost of one missed deadline can be a full year of delayed education. 40% of surveyed students reported missing at least one deadline in the past year.

#### 2.2.4 Application Overwhelm
Each scholarship requires a unique combination of essays, documents, and form fields. Students juggle 15-30 applications simultaneously with no system to track what's complete, what's missing, and what's next.

#### 2.2.5 Essay & Document Burden
Writing a compelling statement of purpose for each application is exhausting. Most students don't have access to editors, mentors, or professional guidance. They submit generic essays that don't stand out. The average serious applicant writes 5-8 unique essays per application cycle.

#### 2.2.6 Trust Deficit
Many scholarship listings online are outdated, expired, or misleading. Students can't tell which opportunities are real and which are clickbait. They need verified, current information they can act on with confidence. 35% of aggregator listings we audited had broken links or expired deadlines.

## 3. Our Solution

Zawadi is an **AI-powered scholarship matching and application management platform** built specifically for African students. We combine:

### 3.1 Curated Scholarship Database
- Every listing verified for African eligibility
- Direct application links — no aggregator dead ends
- Deadline tracking with urgency indicators (🔴 Within 14 days / 🟡 Within 30 days / 🟢 Within 90 days / 🔵 Rolling)
- Continuously updated by the Zawadi Bot, which hunts for new opportunities daily
- **32 verified scholarships at launch, targeting 500 by Q4 2026**

### 3.2 AI-Powered Matching
- Profile-based matching: country, degree level, field of study, destination preference
- Match scores (0-100%) so students know which scholarships to prioritize
- Smart recommendations that improve with every interaction

### 3.3 Application Management Dashboard
- Track every application through stages: Not Started → Saved → Drafting → Ready → Applied → Interview → Awarded → Rejected
- Color-coded deadline urgency
- Document gap analysis — know exactly what's missing before you submit
- Stats dashboard: total, applied, drafting, urgent, strong matches

### 3.4 AI Essay Generator
- 3-stage pipeline: Draft → Critique & Rewrite → Final Polish
- Learns your voice from uploaded writing samples (coming soon)
- Generates personalized statements of purpose, motivation letters, and essays tailored to each scholarship
- Supports: Personal Statement, SOP, Motivation Letter, Leadership Essay, Study Plan

### 3.5 Document Intelligence
- Auto-detects document types (CV, transcript, passport, certificate, etc.)
- Flags gaps before you apply
- Upload once, reuse across applications
- AI-powered analysis and improvement suggestions

### 3.6 Auto-Apply Engine
- Automatically fills scholarship application forms using your profile and documents
- Saves as draft when fields can't be filled — shows exactly what's missing
- Batch auto-apply to multiple scholarships at once

## 10. Guiding Principles

1. **Africa-first, always.** Every feature decision starts with: "Does this serve African students better?"
2. **Verify before we publish.** No scholarship goes live without confirmed eligibility, deadline, and direct application link.
3. **Build for the student who has nothing.** Our free tier must be genuinely useful — not a teaser.
4. **Earn trust through transparency.** Show match scores, show data sources, show when information was last verified.
5. **Move fast, but don't break trust.** Ship quickly, but never at the expense of accuracy.
6. **Every link must work.** A broken link is a broken promise. We validate every URL.
7. **Learn from outcomes.** Track which applications succeed and fail. Use that data to help the next student.
8. **Privacy is a right, not a feature.** Collect only what we need, protect it properly, and let users delete it.
