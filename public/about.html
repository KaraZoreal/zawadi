About Techsari Zawadi

Building the future of scholarship discovery for Africa

"Zawadi exists to ensure that no African student misses a life-changing scholarship opportunity because of information gaps, missed deadlines, or lack of application support."

Our Story

Techsari was founded with a simple belief: talent is evenly distributed across Africa, but opportunity is not. Every year, hundreds of thousands of brilliant African students compete for scholarships worldwide — but too many lose out not because they aren't qualified, but because they couldn't find the right opportunity, missed a deadline, or submitted a generic application.

Zawadi (Swahili for "gift") was built to change that. We combine AI-powered tools with a curated, verified scholarship database to give every African student a fair shot at the education they deserve.

What We Believe

  • Africa-first, always. Every feature is designed with African students in mind.
  • Verify before we publish. No scholarship goes live without confirmed eligibility and a working link.
  • Build for the student who has nothing. Our free tier must be genuinely useful — not a teaser.
  • Privacy is a right. We collect only what we need and protect it like it's our own.
  • Every link must work. A broken link is a broken promise.

Our Team

Zawadi is built by Techsari, a technology company specializing in AI-powered solutions for Africa. Our team combines expertise in artificial intelligence, software engineering, and education — united by a mission to unlock opportunity through technology.

The Technology

Zawadi is powered by:

  • AI Scholarship Matching — Profile-based matching with relevance scores
  • AI Essay Generator — 3-stage pipeline (Draft → Critique → Polish) using large language models
  • Zawadi Bot — Automated AI agent that discovers and verifies new scholarships daily
  • Document Intelligence — AI-powered document analysis and gap detection
  • Auto-Apply Engine — Automated scholarship application form filling
