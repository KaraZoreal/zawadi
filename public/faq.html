Frequently Asked Questions

Techsari Zawadi Scholarship Portal

What is Zawadi?

Zawadi is an AI-powered scholarship discovery and application management platform built specifically for African students. We help you find scholarships you're eligible for, track your applications, generate AI-assisted essays, and manage your documents — all in one place.

Is Zawadi free?

Yes! Zawadi has a generous free tier (Explorer plan) that includes unlimited scholarship browsing, application tracking, 3 documents, and 3 AI-generated essays per day. You only upgrade when you need more.

How much do paid plans cost?

  • Scholar Plus: $5/month or $50/year — unlimited documents, 15 essays/day, premium filters
  • Application Pro: $12/month or $120/year — 30 essays/day, 20 auto-applies/day, document analysis
  • Mentor Review: $29/month or $290/year — 50 essays/day, priority support, expert essay review

All prices are shown in USD and your local currency. Payments are processed via Paystack in Kenyan Shillings (KES).

Which countries do you cover?

Zawadi covers all 54 African countries. Our scholarship database is filtered specifically for African student eligibility. We currently have students from Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa, Ethiopia, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda, and more.

How do you find scholarships?

Our AI-powered Zawadi Bot searches for scholarships daily across university websites, government portals, and foundation pages. Every scholarship is verified for African eligibility and must have a direct application link — no dead ends.

Can I trust the scholarship listings?

Yes. Every scholarship in our database is verified. We check: African eligibility, current deadlines, and direct application links. We reject listings from aggregator sites that don't link to the official application page. Our current dead link rate is under 2%.

What is the AI essay generator?

Our AI essay generator helps you write compelling statements of purpose, motivation letters, and personal essays. It uses a 3-stage pipeline: Draft → Critique & Rewrite → Final Polish. You provide the prompt and details about the scholarship, and the AI creates a personalized essay you can review and edit.

How does the application tracker work?

For each scholarship, you can track your progress through 8 stages: Not Started → Saved → Drafting → Ready → Applied → Interview → Awarded → Rejected. You can set priority levels, add notes, and see at a glance which applications need attention.

What documents can I upload?

You can upload CVs, resumes, transcripts, certificates, motivation letters, statements of purpose, reference letters, passports, financial evidence, admission letters, essays, and other application documents. Free tier: 3 documents. Paid tiers: unlimited.

How do I pay?

We use Paystack for payments. You can pay with debit/credit cards, mobile money (M-Pesa), or bank transfer — all in your local currency. Payment is secure and PCI-DSS compliant.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel anytime. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period. There are no cancellation fees.

How is my data protected?

We take your privacy seriously. Passwords are hashed with scrypt and never stored in plaintext. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+). We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Who built Zawadi?

Zawadi is built by Techsari, a technology company focused on building AI-powered tools for Africa.
